# Simple_2D_Scene
Created for CS3113

This is my Project for a Simple 2D Scene: Rayquaza's Capture

Here, I have 4 Different Objects:
Skypillar- The background of my scene (does not move)
Rayquaza- The pokemon that moves in a figure 8 pattern (cos and faster sin) before being caught by the Masterball (scaling down into it)
Masterball- A ball that moves in a curved pattern (modified sin) while spinning in the air (rotating) before hitting Rayquaza and falling to the floor, catching Rayquaza successfully
Star- A star that comes out of the Masterball to indicate Rayquaza was caught. It moves in an S shaped pattern (cosine wave moving upwards)

Here, my Rayquaza transforms in relation to my Masterball, scaling down into the Masterball to get caught

My background's sky color alternates between shades of blue to indicate a stormy sky.
